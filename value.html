What’s my home worth?

There’s no instant valuation on this site. Here’s how Hunter sellers can build a realistic picture of value, and where to look up recent local sales.

See recent local sales

Sold prices for similar homes nearby are the best starting point. Premier Estate Agents (New Lambton) publishes a free recent-sales lookup: pick your suburb and it shows homes that agency has sold there or close by.

Open the recent-sales lookup

The lookup is on a separate site. Its results are past sales shown as a guide only. They are not an appraisal or a valuation of your home.

Three ways to gauge value

  • Recent sold evidence. Look for homes that are similar in type, land size, condition and location and that sold recently. Asking prices on current listings are not sold evidence.
  • Appraisals from local agents. An agent’s appraisal is an opinion of the likely selling range. In NSW the agent’s estimated selling price must be reasonable and backed by evidence such as comparable sales, so ask to see it.
  • A formal valuation. If you need a figure for a lender, a family law matter or an estate, a qualified property valuer (for example, a Certified Practising Valuer) can prepare a formal valuation report. That is a different service from an agent’s appraisal.
