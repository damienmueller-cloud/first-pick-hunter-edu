Selling costs and timeline — Hunter NSW

Budgets and calendars vary by property and agent agreement. Treat this as a planning framework, then confirm numbers in writing.

Cost categories to budget

  • Agent commission — confirm % or fee structure in the agency agreement.
  • Marketing — photography, listing portals, print or social as agreed.
  • Preparation (renovate vs present?) — cleaning, minor repairs, sometimes staging (optional).
  • Legal / conveyancing — seller’s solicitor or conveyancer fees.
  • Adjustments — rates, water and strata adjustments at settlement.

Typical timeline stages

  1. Appraisal and decision to sell
  2. Preparation and photography
  3. Campaign / opens (often 2–6+ weeks depending on method)
  4. Exchange of contracts
  5. Settlement (commonly around 6 weeks after exchange unless otherwise agreed)

Hunter tip

Coastal Port Stephens, lake-side Lake Macquarie and inland Cessnock / Maitland corridors can show different seasonality. Ask any agent you interview how timing affects your suburb — not a generic national average.

Selling an investment or rental? Tax outcomes (including capital gains) are separate from agent fees and marketing costs — confirm with your accountant or solicitor.
