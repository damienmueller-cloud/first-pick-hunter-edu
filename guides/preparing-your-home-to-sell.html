Preparing your home to sell

Preparation does not mean spending a fortune. It means removing friction for buyers and presenting a property that shows well.

Flood or bushfire disclosure? See flood & bushfire disclosure for Hunter sellers (education only — not legal or planning advice). Approaching settlement? See settlement day checklist for sellers. Exchange or cooling-off? See cooling-off & exchange for sellers (education only — not legal advice).

Clarify your timeline first

ASAP, 1–3 months, or researching only? Timeline shapes whether you do light tidy-up, targeted repairs, or hold for a better window.

Presentation basics

Declutter, finish small repairs, deep-clean wet areas, maximise light, tidy gardens. Ask your eventual agent what matters most for your property type.

What to discuss locally

Paint, staging or renovation trade-offs differ by price band and suburb across the five Hunter councils. Get locality-specific advice before big spends. Documents & logistics

Rates notices, strata info if applicable, access for photos and opens — good logistics shorten the path from decision to campaign.
